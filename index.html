Why Drawn verifies identity

Drawn uses Stripe Identity to verify that every user is who they say they are and meets the minimum age requirement for the tracks they've selected. This is not optional — it's foundational to the product we're building.

Your identity document is processed securely by Stripe and is never stored on Drawn's servers. We receive only a verification result — not your document. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
